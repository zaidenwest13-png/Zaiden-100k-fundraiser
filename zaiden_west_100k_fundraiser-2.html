Why I'm Doing It

Young umpires and officials are an important part of sport. They can face pressure, criticism and difficult moments while learning and developing. I want this challenge to encourage people to talk about mental health, support young officials and make sport a positive place for everyone.

This fundraiser is about showing young umpires that their wellbeing matters — on the field, on the court and away from sport too.
